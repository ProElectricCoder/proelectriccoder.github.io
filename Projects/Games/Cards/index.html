How To Play

Objective

Get rid of every card in your hand. Whoever is still holding cards when everyone else has escaped is stuck — that's the loser.

The Deal

A standard 52-card deck is shuffled and dealt out evenly to every player (3–10, including you). Whoever holds the Ace of Spades opens the game.

Opening Trick

The Ace of Spades leads the very first trick. Everyone else throws their highest Spade; anyone with no Spades throws their highest Club, or their highest card if they have neither. This one trick is special — every card played in it is removed from the game for good. Nobody picks it up.

Leading a Trick

After the opening trick, whoever leads plays their lowest card. If their lowest card is a 2 and they're holding an unbroken run right above it (like 2-3-4), they lead the top of that run instead — dumping as many low cards as possible in one go.

Following Suit

Everyone else must follow the suit that was led, if they can.

Penalty Cut

Can't follow suit? You're forced to throw your highest card overall — a penalty for being caught without the suit.

Who Picks Up

Once everyone's played, whoever threw the highest card of the suit that was led has to scoop the entire trick into their hand. That player leads the next trick.

Escaping

Play your last card and survive the trick without picking up, and you're out — safely shed. Play continues until one unlucky player is left holding cards.

How the bots play

Bots follow the rules mechanically: lead their lowest card (or a low run), play defensively in the middle of a trick (their highest card that still stays under the table), and — as the last to act — throw their highest card of the suit, since by then they can see exactly what's safe. You get to choose for yourself within the same constraints.

Playing Online

Sign in, then either host a room (pick how many total seats — any left empty when you start fill in with bots) or join one with a room code. The host's browser runs the game; everyone else's hand stays private to them.
